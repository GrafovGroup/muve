Service questions

Questions about warehouse & inventory relocation.

Can warehouse inventory move in phases?

Yes. We can assess phased transfers by stock group or warehouse zone. Your dispatch and receiving teams agree cut-offs, stock priorities and handover responsibilities before the schedule is finalized.

Does warehouse moving include racking and machinery?

Standard stock transport does not automatically include rack dismantling, anchoring, machinery rigging or commissioning. List those items separately so the required specialist scope can be assessed.

A clearer brief. A better move plan.

What should I prepare for a commercial moving quote?

Share your origin and destination cities, preferred dates, and the items being moved. Include floors, elevators, loading docks, access restrictions, and any packing or storage needs. An approximate inventory is enough to start the conversation.

What happens after I request a move plan?

Müve reviews your project, clarifies any missing details with you, and confirms availability. The proposal sets out the scope, route, responsibilities, and included services before you book.

Can I request a service without a complete move?

Yes. You can enquire about commercial storage, furniture installation, or office decommissioning. Enter the project address in both location fields when the work takes place at one site. Availability and the scope of work will be confirmed.
