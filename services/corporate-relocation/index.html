Employee relocation services across Canada

One household. Two points of contact.

Each employee has a different starting point. We work within your approved relocation policy, plan the packing and household move, and align delivery with the employee’s start date. Your HR team has a direct contact at Müve for the move.

Destination support and storage depend on location and availability. Immigration, tax, and real estate advice must be provided by appropriately qualified professionals.

  • Employee household packing, moving, and delivery
  • Unpacking and temporary storage options
  • Move schedules aligned with employment start dates

How this service works

Connect the home move to the new role.

01

Agree the employer’s scope

HR confirms the approved packing, transport, storage, and unpacking services. Identify who authorizes changes and which costs need separate employee approval.

02

Build the household schedule

The employee supplies the household inventory, property access, and possession dates. Packing and delivery are planned around those dates and the work start date.

03

Settle the delivery details

Confirm who will receive the shipment, where rooms and larger pieces are located, and which essentials need to be unpacked first. Record any outstanding delivery items.

Service questions

For HR teams and relocating employees.

Can you work within an employer relocation policy?

Yes. We plan the household move around your approved services, budget, authorization process, and employee timing. Your proposal separates employer-paid services from any approved extras. Please keep private HR records out of the enquiry form.

What if the employee cannot move into the new home immediately?

Give us the move-out date and expected possession date. We can review temporary storage and a later household delivery, with access, timing, and charges confirmed in the proposal.

Plan your project

A clearer brief. A better move plan.

What should I prepare for a commercial moving quote?

Share your origin and destination cities, preferred dates, and the items being moved. Include floors, elevators, loading docks, access restrictions, and any packing or storage needs. An approximate inventory is enough to start the conversation.

What happens after I request a move plan?

Müve reviews your project, clarifies any missing details with you, and confirms availability. The proposal sets out the scope, route, responsibilities, and included services before you book.

Can I request a service without a complete move?

Yes. You can enquire about commercial storage, furniture installation, or office decommissioning. Enter the project address in both location fields when the work takes place at one site. Availability and the scope of work will be confirmed.
