Commercial storage services across Canada

Store for the order you’ll need things back.

We record the items going into storage, agree their handling and access needs, and plan collection and redelivery. Separate the pieces you need first so we can release them in the order your new space is ready.

We confirm the storage location, environmental conditions, security arrangements, insurance terms, and access rules in your proposal. Hazardous, regulated, or temperature-sensitive goods require specialist review.

Service questions

Access, timing, and release.

Can stored goods be delivered in stages?

Yes. We can plan staged redelivery around your access windows and the agreed storage terms. Tell us which inventory groups you need first, the target dates, and who can authorize their release.

How is commercial storage priced?

The inventory volume, duration, handling needs, collection and delivery work, and frequency of access affect the proposal. Staged releases or additional visits should be identified before storage begins.

Plan your project

A clearer brief. A better move plan.

What should I prepare for a commercial moving quote?

Share your origin and destination cities, preferred dates, and the items being moved. Include floors, elevators, loading docks, access restrictions, and any packing or storage needs. An approximate inventory is enough to start the conversation.

What happens after I request a move plan?

Müve reviews your project, clarifies any missing details with you, and confirms availability. The proposal sets out the scope, route, responsibilities, and included services before you book.

Can I request a service without a complete move?

Yes. You can enquire about commercial storage, furniture installation, or office decommissioning. Enter the project address in both location fields when the work takes place at one site. Availability and the scope of work will be confirmed.
