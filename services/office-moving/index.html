A floor plan becomes a move sequence.

We start with your floor plans, inventory, access conditions, and operational deadlines. Then we pack and label your contents, move them to the new space, and place furniture and workstations according to your approved layout.

IT disconnection, electrical work, and specialist installations require a separate scope with your internal team or a qualified specialist.

Service questions

Planning around your workplace.

Can an office move be completed over a weekend?

We plan weekend and phased office moves around your inventory, building access, and technical dependencies. We’ll confirm a realistic schedule with you before booking.

What should we include for an office-moving quote?

Start with workstation counts, furniture quantities, both addresses, floor levels, and loading access. Add packing requirements, the target reopening date, and any temporary storage or phased delivery needs.

A clearer brief. A better move plan.

What should I prepare for a commercial moving quote?

Share your origin and destination cities, preferred dates, and the items being moved. Include floors, elevators, loading docks, access restrictions, and any packing or storage needs. An approximate inventory is enough to start the conversation.

What happens after I request a move plan?

Müve reviews your project, clarifies any missing details with you, and confirms availability. The proposal sets out the scope, route, responsibilities, and included services before you book.

Can I request a service without a complete move?

Yes. You can enquire about commercial storage, furniture installation, or office decommissioning. Enter the project address in both location fields when the work takes place at one site. Availability and the scope of work will be confirmed.
