Retail and showroom movers across Canada

Move your displays. Prepare your next opening.

Your retail team identifies stock groups, fragile displays and priority opening items. We label each shipment by destination zone, coordinate mall or building access and place agreed furniture for your merchandising team.

Stock counts are reconciled against the inventory agreed with your team. Fixed signage, electrical displays, wall-mounted fixtures and security systems need separate specialist arrangements.

Included in your agreed scope

  • Stock and display inventory planning
  • Packing of agreed merchandise and accessories
  • Protective handling of freestanding display furniture
  • Mall access and delivery-window coordination
  • Delivery grouped by department or display zone

Service questions

Questions about retail & showroom moving.

Can you move outside store opening hours?

We review evening, early-morning and phased moves against mall rules, access windows and crew availability. The approved schedule is confirmed in the proposal.

Do you dismantle fixed retail fixtures?

Freestanding fixtures may be included following a review of their construction and instructions. Wall-fixed systems, electrical connections and structural work require a separate approved scope.

Plan your project

A clearer brief. A better move plan.

What should I prepare for a commercial moving quote?

Share your origin and destination cities, preferred dates, and the items being moved. Include floors, elevators, loading docks, access restrictions, and any packing or storage needs. An approximate inventory is enough to start the conversation.

What happens after I request a move plan?

Müve reviews your project, clarifies any missing details with you, and confirms availability. The proposal sets out the scope, route, responsibilities, and included services before you book.

Can I request a service without a complete move?

Yes. You can enquire about commercial storage, furniture installation, or office decommissioning. Enter the project address in both location fields when the work takes place at one site. Availability and the scope of work will be confirmed.
