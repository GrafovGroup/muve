Separate ordinary contents from clinical equipment.

We inventory the rooms with your clinical team, pack the agreed furniture and equipment, and sequence delivery around your return-to-use plan. Equipment needing manufacturer input, specialist handling, or separate transport is identified before move day.

Patient records, biological materials, hazardous goods, and regulated or calibrated equipment require a separately agreed specialist scope. Equipment commissioning and validation require qualified specialists.

Service questions

Before releasing clinical rooms.

Does a clinic move include medical equipment commissioning?

Commissioning, calibration, validation, patient records, and regulated materials are not assumed to be part of standard transport. Those responsibilities must be agreed with qualified specialists and the clinical team before the move.

How can we reduce disruption to patient appointments?

Identify the rooms that must reopen first, the last appointment in each area, and any commissioning dependencies. We can then assess whether separate packing and delivery phases fit the practice’s access and operating schedule.

Plan your project

A clearer brief. A better move plan.

What should I prepare for a commercial moving quote?

Share your origin and destination cities, preferred dates, and the items being moved. Include floors, elevators, loading docks, access restrictions, and any packing or storage needs. An approximate inventory is enough to start the conversation.

What happens after I request a move plan?

Müve reviews your project, clarifies any missing details with you, and confirms availability. The proposal sets out the scope, route, responsibilities, and included services before you book.

Can I request a service without a complete move?

Yes. You can enquire about commercial storage, furniture installation, or office decommissioning. Enter the project address in both location fields when the work takes place at one site. Availability and the scope of work will be confirmed.
