Office furniture installation across Canada

From component lists to usable workstations.

We review your furniture systems, quantities, and floor plan, then deliver, assemble, and position the agreed pieces. We check the completed layout with you and record any outstanding items in the final walkthrough.

Electrical connections, wall fixing, structural alterations, and manufacturer-specific work require a separately agreed scope with qualified specialists. Product compatibility, missing parts, and warranty requirements are assessed before assembly.

Service questions

Before the components arrive.

Can existing furniture be reused in the new layout?

Provide product details, quantities, condition, and the proposed floor plan. Reuse depends on fit, available parts, manufacturer requirements, and the installation scope. Technical or building works are reviewed separately.

What if some parts are missing or damaged?

Identify known shortages when sharing the furniture schedule. We can record affected workstations and discuss the assembly sequence, but compatible replacement parts and manufacturer requirements must be resolved before those pieces can be completed.

Plan your project

A clearer brief. A better move plan.

What should I prepare for a commercial moving quote?

Share your origin and destination cities, preferred dates, and the items being moved. Include floors, elevators, loading docks, access restrictions, and any packing or storage needs. An approximate inventory is enough to start the conversation.

What happens after I request a move plan?

Müve reviews your project, clarifies any missing details with you, and confirms availability. The proposal sets out the scope, route, responsibilities, and included services before you book.

Can I request a service without a complete move?

Yes. You can enquire about commercial storage, furniture installation, or office decommissioning. Enter the project address in both location fields when the work takes place at one site. Availability and the scope of work will be confirmed.
