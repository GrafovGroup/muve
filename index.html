A few things you might be wondering

Before you make your move.

Where does Müve operate?

We accept local and interprovincial moving enquiries across Canada. Toronto and the GTA remain our operating base. Availability is confirmed for your locations, dates, and service requirements; remote and northern routes also depend on transport access and season.

What types of moves does Müve handle?

We move offices, employees, clinic contents, and IT equipment. Our services also include commercial storage, furniture installation, and office decommissioning. We’ll tailor the work to your inventory, premises, and schedule.

Can you plan a move outside business hours?

We plan evening, weekend, and phased moves around your business. We’ll confirm the schedule with you after reviewing building access, the inventory, and any specialist work.

How is a commercial move priced?

We price your move around the inventory, access, distance, packing, handling needs, and schedule. Your quote sets out the included services, assumptions, and any separately priced work before you book.

Who will manage my move?

You work directly with Müve from the first conversation through the final walkthrough. We plan the move, handle the agreed packing and transport, and place your items at the destination. Your move plan also identifies any technical tasks that need your IT team or a qualified specialist.

Do you handle local and interprovincial moves?

Yes. Whether you are moving within one city or between provinces, share the pickup and delivery addresses, inventory, and preferred dates. We’ll confirm the packing, transport, delivery, and any storage or installation included in your move plan.

What should I prepare for a commercial moving quote?

Share your origin and destination cities, preferred dates, and the items being moved. Include floors, elevators, loading docks, access restrictions, and any packing or storage needs. An approximate inventory is enough to start the conversation.

What happens after I request a move plan?

Müve reviews your project, clarifies any missing details with you, and confirms availability. The proposal sets out the scope, route, responsibilities, and included services before you book.

Can I request a service without a complete move?

Yes. You can enquire about commercial storage, furniture installation, or office decommissioning. Enter the project address in both location fields when the work takes place at one site. Availability and the scope of work will be confirmed.
