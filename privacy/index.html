Privacy & enquiries.

The enquiry form collects your name, company, contact details, move locations, proposed timing, and the project information you choose to provide. This information is used to assess and respond to your relocation request.

We use Formspree to process, store, and deliver enquiries to Müve so we can respond to your request. Do not include patient records, employee files, financial information, passwords, or other sensitive material. If your move requires an external specialist, we’ll agree any sharing of project details with you as part of planning the work.

To ask about your enquiry, request a correction, or request deletion, email info@muve.ca or call (833) 698-MUVE (6883) and include the name and email address used for your enquiry and the date you sent it. This website does not use advertising or analytics cookies. Fonts are requested from Google Fonts; those requests disclose connection information such as your IP address to that provider.
